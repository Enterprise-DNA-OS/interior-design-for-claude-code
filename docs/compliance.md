# Evidence checks for a New Zealand design studio

Sources checked 2026-10-03. These are record prompts, not a certificate or legal assessment. They do not test product safety, building consent requirements, electrical suitability or access-control effectiveness. Australian projects need jurisdiction-specific rules scoped before use.

| Rule | What the command checks | Basis and limit |
|---|---|---|
| SPEC-01 | A selection has specification text | House evidence rule supporting a record of what was described. Text presence does not establish suitability. |
| CGA-01 | Received quantities have a condition note | House evidence rule supporting delivery and condition review. A person must inspect the goods. |
| CGA-02 | Product issues have a recorded resolution | House evidence rule supporting follow-up. A filled field does not prove an adequate remedy. |
| IPP5-01 | A project has an access and backup review date in the last 90 days | The 90-day interval is studio policy, not a statutory deadline. A date alone does not establish security. |

[Consumer Protection's guidance for businesses](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/guidance-for-businesses/complying-with-consumer-laws/obligations-under-the-consumer-guarantees-act) covers household goods, including quality, purpose, description and agreed delivery. It informs the specification, receipt and product-issue evidence prompts. Applicability and any remedy depend on the transaction and facts. No automated rule decides them.

[Privacy Commissioner, Information Privacy Principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) requires reasonable safeguards for personal information. The review date is a reminder for the operator to examine who can access the database, backups and exported documents. It neither implements nor verifies those safeguards.

Current-revision approval before ordering, frozen ordered specifications, receipt quantity caps and keeping project currencies separate are studio controls. They are not described as statutory requirements. Client approval evidence is supplied by the operator and retained with its revision. Imported status text never creates approval evidence.
